WHAT IT DOES

A dashboard built around one question: who's leaving?

Every prediction combines twelve HR data points into a single, explainable risk score — fast enough to run during a 1:1, thorough enough to trust.

Instant Prediction

Enter an employee's profile and get a Likely to Stay / Likely to Leave verdict in under a second.

Confidence Scoring

Every result ships with a probability percentage, not just a binary label — so you know how sure the model is.

Random Forest Core

300 decision trees vote on every prediction, trained on satisfaction, tenure, pay, and workload signals.

Built for HR Workflows

Clean, validated forms designed around the fields HR teams already track in their HRIS.

THE PROCESS

Three steps from data entry to decision

01

Enter employee details

Fill in the twelve fields — demographics, compensation, tenure, and workload — through a validated form.

02

Model evaluates risk

The Random Forest pipeline encodes the inputs and runs them through 300 trained decision trees.

03

Get a scored result

Receive a Likely to Stay / Likely to Leave verdict, a probability score, and the model's key risk drivers.
